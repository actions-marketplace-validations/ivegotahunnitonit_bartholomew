### 3. Permitted Uses (Nominal & Community Use)

You **MAY** use Bartholomew word marks without prior written permission under the following circumstances:

1. **Descriptive & Compatibility Statements**:
   - Stating accurately that your software "works with Bartholomew Guard", "is compatible with BTP v6.3", or "integrates the Keystone Passkey SDK".
2. **Community Code References**:
   - Referring to the official repository (https://github.com/ivegotahunnitonit/bartholomew), packages (npm install btp-guard, pip install btp-guard), or documentation in articles, tutorials, academic research, and social media.
3. **Unmodified Distribution**:
   - Distributing verbatim, unmodified binaries, wheels, tarballs, or VISXInstallers downloaded directly from official Bartholomew releases.

### 4. Prohibited Uses (Brand Dilution & Infringement)

You **MAY NOT** do any of the following without prior express written consent from Bartholomew:
1. **Confusingly Similar Naming**: Using Bartholomew, BTP, or Keystone Passkey as part of the name of your company, project, product, service, domain name, or package distribution (e.g., btp-guard-official, bartholomew-enterprise, btp-cloud).
2. **Re-branded Derivative Forks**: Forking or modifying the source code and distributing it under the name Bartholomew or BTP, or retaining official branding that implies the fork is maintained or verified by Bartholomew.
3. **False Affiliation or Endorsement**: Suggesting or implying that Bartholomew endorses, sponsors, certifies, or is affiliated with your product, enterprise service, or company.
4. **Altering or Commercializing Logos**: Modifying the official Bartholomew shield or Keystone logo, combining them with third-party logos, or using them on commercial promotional merchandise without written permission.
5. **Malicious or Deceptive Registry Packages**: Publishing typo-squatted, shadow, or deceptive packages on npm, PyPI, VS Code Marketplace, or Open VSX using Bartholomew marks.
